What is Vaping?

Vaping is the act of inhaling smoke from an e-cigarette, or more commonly known as a vape, which heats a liquid to create an aerosol (a mixture of liquid chemicals in the form of a gas) that is inhaled. Over time, vape devices have been designed to be small, easy to carry around, and more appealing to younger people like high school students, . Bright colours and flavours like berry, mango, and candy can make them seem harmless or fun to try like its just another everyday product to our tamariki.

This is a dangerous tactic used by money hungry corporations to lure the rangatahi into vaping. Recently, in Aotearoa, it has become a growing issue among in our schools across New Zealand, with some as young as early as primary school. However, it is most commonly seen among teenagers/high school students, who can be more vulnerable to becoming dependent on nicotine.

What might start as “just trying it” with your mates can become a dangerous and difficult habit to break without the proper support and guidance from others...
