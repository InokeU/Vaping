What is Vaping?

Vaping is the act of inhaling smoke from an e-cigarette, or more commonly known as a vape. Its become a recent and ongoing problem among the youth group of Aotearoa since the 2010s, with higher statistics showing especially around the high school age group.

What is that smoke that comes out of vapes?

The "smoke" you see come from a vape device is actually called aerosol, and it is actually produced by suspended droplets in air made of different chemicals like nicotine. So it looks like smoke like from a cigarette, but it isn't.

So what actually is in a vape?

It contains nicoteen, a highly addictive substance that is also found in tobacco in normal cigarettes. This substance stimulates the brain and makes it feel good through releasing endorphins, a chemical related to the pleasure centers in our brains.

Effects on the Brain

Nicotine can alter your brain chemistry, affecting areas involved in learning, memory, and impulse control. It can also rewire your vulnerable brain to become dependent on the vape.

Effects on the Lungs

Inhaling the aerosol from vapes can cause irritation in your lungs, which could lead to inflammation in some cases.
