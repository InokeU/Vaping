What is Vaping?

Vaping is the act of inhaling smoke from an e-cigarette, or more commonly known as a vape, which heats a liquid to create an aerosol (a mixture of liquid chemicals in the form of a gas) that is inhaled. It contains nicoteen, a highly addictive substance that is also found in tobacco in normal cigarettes. This substance stimulates the brain and makes it feel good through releasing endorphins, a chemical related to the pleasure centers in our brains.

Over time, vape devices have been designed to be small, easy to carry around, and more appealing to younger people like high school students, . Bright colours and flavours like berry, mango, and candy can make them seem harmless or fun to try like its just another everyday product to our tamariki.

This is a dangerous tactic used by money hungry corporations to lure the rangatahi into vaping. Recently, in Aotearoa, it has become a growing issue among in our schools across New Zealand, with some as young as early as primary school. However, it is most commonly seen among teenagers/high school students, who can be more vulnerable to becoming dependent on nicotine.

What might start as “just trying it” with your mates can become a dangerous and difficult habit to break without the proper support and guidance from others...

What are the effects of vaping?

Vaping can alter your brain chemistry with continued use over time. It is especially effective on the teenage brain as it continues developing into the mid-20s, particularly in the areas involved in making decisions, learning new things, and paying attention. Nicotine can interfere with this development and can also make the brain more likely to depend on the nicotine over time.

Vaping can also cause problems in your lungs like popcorn lung, which is a condition that you do not want to get just because you were smoking flavored poison.

Because the nicotine can boost a persons mood for a short while, the crash down can hit harder with prolonged use, causing users to seek more of the vapes just to feel that sweet high again.
