What Is ZeroNicoteens?

ZeroNicoteens is a website based movement which aims to reduce the rates of vaping among the Youth in Aotearoa, New Zealand.

Based in Auckland, this website aims to inform and warn specifically the Youth in New Zealand because of an alarming number of typically High School students and even Intermediate students across New Zealand have admitted to regularly using vapes, and these statistics have shown to be a little higher among the Pasifika Youth, but this website can also apply to just about anyone who stumbles across it with the same problems, or knowing someone with the same problems.

Therefore this website was created to not only address the issues with vaping but also try and steer our target audience toward trying better alternatives that do not involve inhaling toxic chemicals into their lungs for a quick rush of euphoria.

How can you overcome your vaping urges?

In this section you will find better alternatives to vaping that can benefit both your physical and mental health in the long term.

Here are 5 ways you can use to help yourself or a loved one overcome addiction



ALTERNATIVE WAY #1: BUILD A ROUTINE

Build a routine. This means creating things to do that you
repeat on a regular basis to
keep yourself busy with other things rather than vaping.
This can include simple things such as
waking up in the morning and going for a 15 minute walk
to wake up your body and brain, reducing the need
to take a bump off the vape.

ALTERNATIVE WAY #2: REMOVE OR REDUCE TRIGGERS

Remove any triggers from your environment that cause you to vape.
This means throwing vape devices away or locking it
somewhere you'll know you can't easily access.
It also might mean distancing yourself from people
you know who vape regularly around you,
like your mates.

ALTERNATIVE WAY #3: REPLACE CRAVINGS WITH SNACKS AND TOYS

You can help manage your cravings by eating and fidgeting with toys.
These can be snacks like peanuts and sunflower seeds,
because they can also help benefit your body.
And also make sure to keep a fidget toy with you,
to distract yourself when the cravings arise.

ALTERNATIVE WAY #4: SEEK SUPPORT

Seek support where you can.
Find someone you really trust and get them to
support you on your journey every step of the way.
Whether if its someone from your family, or a friend, seek
non-judgemental support from those you can trust in your life.

ALTERNATIVE WAY #5: SEE A HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONAL

For the ultimate non-judgemental support,
seek a healthcare doctor who specialises in treating vaping addictions
because they can provide valuable information
into the long-term effects of vaping.
They will assist through giving you advice and providing
a quitting plan suited to your needs.
