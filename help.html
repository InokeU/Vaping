There are better alternatives to vaping...

Vaping is a very difficult habit to break out of someone's life, especially in teenagers. But there are proven ways to manage it or even keep it away for good. If you are a kiwi and are struggling with vaping, here are some tips that can help with managing your impulses!



  • Pick one of your favorite hobbies and grind it until you master it.
    Don't try to learn it all in one go, try to take it one step at a time, day by day.
  • Go for a walk around your neighbourhood.
    Breathing in the fresh air around you is better than breathing in air from a toxic highlighter.
  • Complete unfinished assessments.
    Because teenagers typically vape due to stress, addressing your sources of stress can help dust some weight off your shoulders. It's not easy, so create a list of subjects which require the most to least attention!
  • Take up going to the gym or run.
    This is a great way to keep yourself fit and active, while also improving lung capacity and extending your body's lifespan.
  • Talk to someone you deeply trust and who will unconditionally support you along in your journey.
    This doesn't have to be immediate family members, but also your best friends, guidance counselors, therapists. The help is there, you just need to be wiling to seek it.


    • The trick here is to occupy yourself with various other things so that you are too busy to act on vaping when the urge hits. Though this may not work for everyone, at least one of these methods can help bring something healthier into people's lives, rather than them vaping and inhaling toxic chemicals when they're bored.
